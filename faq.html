General Questions

IPTV stands for Internet Protocol Television. It delivers television channels, movies, and video content over the internet rather than through traditional cable or satellite. With an IPTV subscription, you can watch thousands of channels on any device connected to the internet.

My IPTV Provider is built specifically for the US market. We offer 80,000+ channels in 4K and HD quality, zero buffering, instant activation, and genuine 24/7 customer support. Our servers are optimized for US viewers and our pricing is among the most competitive in the industry.

No special equipment is required. You just need a device with internet access and an IPTV player app. Our service works on Smart TVs, Amazon Firestick, Android devices, iPhones, iPads, PCs, MAG boxes, Roku, and more.

Setup & Activation

Activation is instant. As soon as your payment is confirmed, you will receive your IPTV subscription credentials. You can start streaming within minutes of your purchase, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

Our IPTV service is compatible with: Smart TVs (Samsung, LG, Sony, and others), Amazon Firestick and Fire TV, Android phones and tablets, iPhones and iPads, Windows PCs and Mac computers, MAG boxes, Roku devices, and Android TV boxes. Essentially any device with internet access and an IPTV app.
